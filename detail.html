FEATURED WORK / COLOUR & FORM

About the Exhibitor

Yuezhou Jiang is a student artist. In this profile, Yuezhou enjoys experimenting with simple shapes, bright colours, and mixed media artwork.

Featured Work: Colour & Form

This imagined exhibition explores how a simple circle, square or brushstroke can communicate a feeling. The goal is to help visitors see everyday colours and shapes in a new way.

Creative Process

Yuezhou begins with small sketches and colour palettes. She then experiments with different arrangements before choosing a final composition. The sample artwork above represents this process.

Speaker Spotlight: Artist Talk

At 12:30 PM in the sample event schedule, Yuezhou shares what inspired her work and answers a few audience questions about creativity.

What Visitors Take Away

The sample exhibition gives visitors a chance to see student artwork, learn about a creative process, and ask the exhibitor questions during the artist talk.
